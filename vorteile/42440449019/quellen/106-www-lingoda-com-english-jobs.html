Become a Teacher

Experience the numerous advantages of teaching online

If you love languages, have experience teaching and have a minimum C2-level in your target language, then teaching with Lingoda is for you.

  • Small classes, motivated students

    Our students love learning - whether as a hobby or to progress in their careers! Teach them everything from language basics to pronunciation in small groups or 1-on-1 private classes.

  • Around the clock flexibility

    With our classes available 24/7, you can create a schedule that works for you and teach as much or as little as you want.

  • Support for our teachers

    Our Teacher Relations Team is available for any questions, feedback or tips, plus ensuring that every teacher is paid on time.

  • Structured curriculum

    Our learning materials are CEFR-aligned. Use them to prepare your classes.

Frequently asked questions

  • To become a teacher with us you need to have:

    • C2 or above level of the language you would like to teach.
    • A certificate to teach your chosen language as a foreign language
    • At least 2-3 years of valuable teaching experience (not necessarily online classes).

  • Lingoda classes are taught on Zoom – an online conferencing platform. Therefore you will need a high speed internet connection, camera and a high quality headset.

    All teachers work with Lingoda on a freelance basis – you must be legally able to do so.

    You need to feel comfortable speaking English.

  • The beauty of Lingoda is that you can book both long-term (months in advance) and short-term (hours in advance). This allows you to have a completely flexible and spontaneous schedule.
