Frequently asked questions

How do remix contests help accelerate my music career as an artist?

They give you direct access to real label opportunities, helping you gain exposure and credibility, and many successful artists got their first breakthrough through remixes.

Are there any age or location restrictions for artists joining a contest?

This depends on the specific contest, but generally you must meet LabelRadar’s minimum age requirements (18+, or younger with parental consent where applicable) and comply with any region-based rules outlined in the contest terms.

How do I know if my remix entry has been reviewed by the judges?

You’ll receive convenient real-time updates through LabelRadar’s notification system, so you always know the status of your submission without needing to check manually.

Why should a label host a remix contest for a new release?

Hosting a remix contest unlocks global creativity around your release, turning your track into a discovery engine that drives buzz and engagement at scale.

How do I host a remix contest?

Labels can host a remix contest using the Contest Manager. Apply for a label account (you can use one just for contests), upload stems, set the rules and deadline, and begin crowdsourcing remix entries from a community of artists.
